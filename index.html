trend-analyzer

Detecting emerging subjects before they reach the mainstream.

trend-analyzer is a private, single-user research tool. It reads publicly available search, news and product data through official APIs, measures how specific subjects accelerate against their own history, and reports the ones that are rising while they are still early.

It is not a public product and it has no users other than its owner. It does not sell data, does not publish findings, and does not collect information about visitors to this page.

What it uses

Approved data sources only — official APIs, RSS feeds and public datasets such as Google Ads keyword metrics (with the owner's own authorisation), Google Trends, Stack Exchange, Hacker News, Product Hunt, Apple and Wikimedia. No scraping and no automated page collection.
